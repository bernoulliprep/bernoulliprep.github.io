Contact

Write to bernoulliprep@icloud.com. We usually reply within a few days. Please tell us your device and app version if something isn't working.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get questions into the app?

The app includes a demo with sample questions for every subject. You can import your own question catalogues as an Excel or CSV table, with pictures: the app includes a template with examples and a step-by-step guide.

Does my progress sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on all devices and iCloud is enabled for ATPL Prep. Pull down to refresh on iPhone and iPad, or use the refresh button on the Mac.

Can the app remind me to study?

Yes. In Settings › Reminder you choose daily or weekly and a time. On days you have already studied, there is no reminder.

How do study groups work?

On the Activity tab, create a group and share the invitation link with your class. Members see each other's display name and the number of questions answered per day, nothing else. You can leave a group at any time.

The creator of my group left. What happens?

A group is stored in the creator's iCloud. If the creator leaves or deletes it, the group ends for everyone; simply create a new one.

How do I delete my data?

In the app's settings use "Reset…", or delete the app. The iCloud copy can be removed in your device settings under Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.

I found a mistake in a question.

Thank you! Please write to us with the subject and question, ideally via the support function in the app's settings.
