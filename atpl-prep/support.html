Frequently asked questions

How do I get questions into the app?

The app includes a demo with sample questions for every subject. You can import your own question catalogues as an Excel or CSV table, or as a ZIP with pictures – Settings › Import › Use a spreadsheet has a template with examples and a step-by-step guide. Or write questions yourself right in the app.

How do I write my own questions?

Go to Settings › Import › Write your own questions, create a question pack and add questions with +. Only subject, question and at least two answers are required; chapter, picture, explanation and handbook pages are optional. New questions show up in their subject right away and on your other devices too. Under Settings › Content › Edit › Share you can share a pack as Excel or CSV, edit it and import it again – your progress is kept.

Does my progress sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on all devices and iCloud is enabled for ATPL Prep. Pull down to refresh on iPhone and iPad, or use the refresh button on the Mac.

Can I continue a session on another device?

Yes. A running or paused session appears on the Training page of your other devices while the device it runs on is online with ATPL Prep open – tap “Take over” to continue right where you left off. To pause a session, end it and choose “Continue later”. This needs iCloud on all devices.

How do I back up my learning data?

In Settings › Sync & backup › Export backup. The backup is one file with your progress and history, optionally with all your content. Restore it with Import backup – it is merged with your current data.

Can the app remind me to study?

Yes. In Settings › Reminder you choose daily or weekly and a time. On days you have already studied, there is no reminder.

How do study groups work?

On the Activity tab, create a group and share the invitation link with your class. Members see each other's display name and the number of questions answered per day, nothing else. You can leave a group at any time.

The creator of my group left. What happens?

A group is stored in the creator's iCloud. If the creator leaves or deletes it, the group ends for everyone; simply create a new one.

How do I delete my data?

In the app's settings use "Reset…", or delete the app. The iCloud copy can be removed in your device settings under Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.

I found a mistake in the app or a demo question.

Thank you! Please write to us, ideally via "Contact support" in the app's settings – it adds your app version and device automatically.
