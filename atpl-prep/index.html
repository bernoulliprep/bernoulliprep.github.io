ATPL-Prep

Exam trainer for the ATPL theory.

Coming soon to the App Store

Features

Practice by subject

All 13 ATPL subjects, sorted by topic, with progress that remembers what you still need to work on.

Exam sessions

Test sessions across a subject or a whole progress test, with a review afterwards.

Handbooks next to the question

Open the matching handbook page straight from a question.

Study groups

Compare daily activity with your class via an invitation link. Only your name and question counts are shared.

Your own content

Import your own question catalogues as CSV and your own PDF handbooks.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

Your progress syncs through your own iCloud. No account, no ads, no tracking.

ATPL-Prep is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with EASA, the LBA or any examination provider. The app includes a free demo; question catalogues and handbooks from your course can be imported.
