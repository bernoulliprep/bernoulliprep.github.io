ATPL Prep

Your own ATPL questions and handbooks, turned into a focused exam trainer – on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Download on the App Store

How it works

1 Add your questions

Import your question catalogue as an Excel or CSV table or as a ZIP with pictures – or write questions right in the app. Add your PDF handbooks too.

2 Practise

Learn by subject, chapter or learning stage, or run an exam with chapters weighted like the real one. Every question can open the matching handbook page.

3 Keep track

See where you stand in every subject, repeat your mistakes and continue on any of your devices.

Features

Your own questions

Import tables or ZIP files, or write questions in the app and keep them in your own question packs – with pictures, chapters, explanations and handbook links. Share them as Excel or CSV and edit them in any spreadsheet app.

Learning stages

Every question moves from New through Wrong, 1× and 2× Correct to Learned. Open learning repeats mistakes sooner and brings learned questions back at growing intervals.

Exam mode

Chapters weighted like the real exam, an optional time limit and optional live feedback. Pause, submit or abort at any time – and review every session in the history.

Your handbooks

Your PDFs sorted by subject and searchable. Explanations and references jump straight to the right page, and every document opens where you stopped reading.

Continue on any device

Start a session on your iPhone and take it over on your iPad or Mac, right where you left off. Progress, history and your selections sync via your own iCloud.

Study groups

Create a private group for your class and share the invite link. Only the number of answered questions is shared – never how many were right.

One purchase for iPhone, iPad and Mac – no subscription, no account, no ads, no tracking. ATPL Prep is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with EASA, the LBA, any authority or flight school. It contains no official exam questions and no question bank of its own: it is built for the questions you import or write. A small demo with sample questions for every subject is included so you can try everything first.
