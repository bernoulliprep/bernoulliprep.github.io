Privacy Policy

ATPL Prep app and this website

In short

ATPL Prep has no user account and no servers of ours. Your learning data stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account with Apple. We have no access to it. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Controller

Jannik Herre, Carl-von-Noorden-Platz 10, 60596 Frankfurt am Main, Germany, bernoulliprep@icloud.com

What data the app creates

The app stores your learning progress and your favourites. This includes, for example, which questions you answered correctly, how long you took and how many questions you answered per day. It also stores the content packs you import, such as question catalogues and handbooks. So that you can continue a session on another of your devices, the app also stores a randomly generated device identifier, the type and name of the device (e.g. “Jannik’s iPad”) and – while the app is open – the state of running and paused sessions.

Sync via iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, the app syncs this data between your devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit in the app's private database, the iCloud key-value store for settings, and the app's folder in iCloud Drive for imported content). The data is stored in your personal iCloud account; Apple processes it on our behalf as the provider of the infrastructure, and we cannot see or read it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or turn off iCloud for ATPL Prep in your device settings, everything stays on the device only. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Learning groups

You can create or join a learning group to compare activity with fellow students. Groups are entirely optional. When you are in a group, the following is visible to all members of that group: the display name you choose and the number of questions you answered per day. No answers, results or other data are shared. A group is stored in the iCloud account of the person who created it and is shared through Apple's iCloud sharing; anyone can join only via the invitation link. You can leave a group at any time, which removes your entry; the creator can remove members and rename the group. The legal basis is Article 6 (1) (b) GDPR, as you use this feature at your own request.

Nothing is sent to us

There is no sign-in with us. We operate no servers for the app to send data to. We collect no usage statistics, use no analytics or tracking tools, and include no advertising networks.

Contacting us by email

If you write to us through the support function or directly by email, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply. Emails sent from the app's support function contain the app version, device model and system version so we can help you more quickly; you can delete these lines before sending. The legal basis is Article 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry. We delete the message once it has been dealt with and no retention obligation applies.

Our website

Our website bernoulliprep.github.io is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the time of access and your browser information in server log files to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is Article 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in a secure, reliable website. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. It sets no cookies, loads no external fonts and uses no analytics.

Legal texts in the app

When you open the legal notice, the privacy policy or the other legal texts in the app, it retrieves the current version of these texts from our website (bernoulliprep.github.io) and saves it on your device. GitHub processes your IP address and the time of access, as described under "Our website". No other data is transmitted. If there is no connection, the app shows the version it already has. The legal basis is Article 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in showing you the current version of these texts.

App Store and purchases

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. Which data Apple processes in that context is outside our control; please refer to Apple's privacy information. The same applies to paid content: purchase and billing are handled by Apple. From Apple we receive only aggregated sales and payout reports from which individuals cannot be identified.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; the authority responsible for us is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Postfach 3163, 65021 Wiesbaden, Germany. You can delete your learning data yourself at any time: in the app's settings via "Reset…", by deleting the app, and for the iCloud copy in your device settings under Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage › ATPL Prep.

Changes

If the app gains new features that process data differently, this policy will be updated beforehand.
